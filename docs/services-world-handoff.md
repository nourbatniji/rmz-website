# Services lens — "shared world" image system: design handoff

The labelled overview with the seven target windows: `docs/services-world-handoff-overview.webp`. The artwork in `public/media/services-world/` is **temporary calibration art** generated by `scripts/gen-services-world-assets.mjs`; it is not client work and not final.

## How it works (so the artwork can be briefed correctly)
One **overview** image shows all seven services together. Each service has a **target point** on it and a **zoom**. Selecting a service flies the lens camera to the target, then hands over to that service's **dedicated image**, which sits exactly on the part of the overview the glass shows at arrival (the "window"). Between services the camera pulls out a little, moves, and enters the next window — the overview is never a stop of its own.

## Technical requirements (the renderer relies on these)
- Overview: **one image**. Any aspect ratio works (it is cover-fitted into the square glass, never stretched), but only a square overview shows everything at zoom 1; other ratios crop the sides at zoom 1.
- Seven dedicated images. They are placed in the window (square, side = overview side ÷ zoom, centred on the target) and cover-fitted with the focal point, so **square files are exact**; non-square files are cropped to a square around `focal`.
- For a seamless hand-over each dedicated image must depict the **same content as its window of the overview** (same framing, same scale) — only sharper/more detailed. If it depicts something different, the cross-fade will show two unrelated pictures.
- Targets are normalised against the overview file (0–1 of its own width/height). Keep every window inside the image: target ± 0.5/zoom must lie within 0–1 (the camera clamps otherwise, which shifts the window and breaks alignment).
- Formats accepted by the dashboard/uploader: WebP, JPEG, PNG, AVIF (SVG/GIF accepted but not suitable). Max file size 10 MB. Plain `<img>`: files are served as uploaded (no automatic resizing).
- No transparency needed (the glass has a dark backing). No text or logos are drawn by the system any more; if the artwork contains text it will be cropped, tinted by the aperture blades and not accessible.
- Everything is clipped by the circular glass and covered by the aperture blades: parts of the window near the corners and, at small apertures, near the edge are hidden. The aperture at rest per service: Branding 94 %, Creative Strategy 66 %, Content Production 84 %, Social Media 74 %, Design 96 %, Paid Media 60 %, Web Experiences 80 % (opening overview: 96 %). The visible opening is a regular octagon whose inscribed circle is that share of the glass diameter.

## Recommendations (design choices, not enforced)
- Overview: **1:1**, **3200 × 3200 px** (≥ 2400). Largest glass is ≈ 541 CSS px (1083 px at 2× screens). The overview is visible at up to ≈ zoom × that size while the camera moves, so 3200 px keeps it sharp to ≈ 3× zoom at 2× screens; the last part of an arrival is covered by the dedicated image anyway.
- Dedicated images: **1:1**, **1600 × 1600 px** (minimum 1083 × 1083 for 2× screens, 1625 for 3× screens). Subject centred; keep the key subject within the middle ~60 % (the blades and the glass edge cover the rest at low apertures).
- Zoom 3–4.5× per service gave good results in the prototype; keep neighbouring windows from overlapping so a zoom into one service does not show another service's subject.
- File size: overview ≈ 400–700 KB, each dedicated image ≈ 150–350 KB (WebP/AVIF, quality ≈ 80). All eight are loaded.
- Provide the seven target coordinates and zooms with the files (see `world-config.ts`); a labelled overview with the seven window rectangles is the clearest brief (`docs/services-world-handoff-overview.webp`).

## Prototype settings
`src/components/lens-orbit/world/world-config.ts` — typed overview, aperture and per-service `{ target, zoom, focal, image }`.

## Homepage integration (Services lens)
- Content: `lens.overview` (one image) and, per service, `lens.items[i].world = { target, zoom, focal, image }`. The old nine slots (`lens.images`) and per-service `scene` stay in saved content for compatibility but are no longer drawn or edited.
- Older saved content with no shared-world data falls back to the built-in (temporary calibration) overview and views, so the lens is never blank; a service without its own image keeps the camera on the zoomed overview.
- Dashboard: Services showcase → overview image; per service: service image, zoom (1.5–6×), target picker on the overview (yellow frame = the window shown at that zoom), focal point picker.
- Scroll: desktop track is 1.5 × the previous one (`--orb-extra`: clamp(5340px, 780svh, 7380px), 3× the original clamp(1780px, 260svh, 2460px)); holds are longer (0.8 timeline units, last 1.2, previously 0.6). Phones keep their own 1500 px track.
